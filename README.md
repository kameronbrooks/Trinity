# Trinity

A [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** that adds a **nuclear bomb**, plus the whole uranium production chain you need to build one: mining, milling, enrichment, and assembly.

Arm it with flint and steel or a redstone signal, take cover, and watch it carve a crater, throw up a towering mushroom cloud, incinerate and level everything in the blast radius, and leave the ruins soaked in green radiation.

## Features

- **Nuclear Bomb block** — armed like TNT (flint & steel, a fire charge, or a redstone signal), with a short beeping fuse.
- **Blinding flash** and a huge, low **boom** at detonation.
- **Expanding shockwave** that levels and scorches everything within the blast radius:
  - carves a **crater**,
  - fuses **sand into green glass** (trinitite — exactly what the real 1945 Trinity test produced),
  - chars **wood into coal blocks**,
  - blackens **stone into blackstone/basalt** and **grass/dirt into coarse dirt** (scorched earth),
  - burns away foliage and scatters **fires** across flammable surfaces.
- **Towering mushroom cloud** built from a custom, oversized smoke particle.
- **Every living thing** in range is killed (creative/spectator players are spared).
- **Lingering green radiation cloud** poisons the crater for minutes — a custom `Radiation` effect stacked with nausea, weakness, hunger, and mining fatigue.
- **Water flows back into the crater** naturally after the blast.

## Performance

A wall of TNT freezes or crashes the game because each primed-TNT *entity* runs its own explosion with block drops, physics, and lighting recalcs. Trinity avoids that entirely: a single blast runs on the server tick loop and does its work in **bounded slices spread across many ticks** — an expanding shockwave — editing blocks with **neighbour updates, block physics, and item drops suppressed**. The whole size of the blast is a single constant (`Blast.RADIUS`).

## Building a bomb

You can't just craft one: a bomb takes a ten-phase production chain, starting from ore you have to dig out of the deepslate. The **Trinity advancement tab** walks you through it, and finding your first uranium unlocks every recipe in the recipe book.

| # | Phase | What you make | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mining | **Uranium Ore** → **Raw Uranium** | Glowing, emerald-like ore, about as rare as diamonds, mostly in deepslate (Y −64 to 32). Iron pickaxe or better; Fortune works. |
| 2 | Milling | **Yellowcake** | Smelt raw uranium (furnace or blast furnace). |
| 3 | Conversion | **Uranium Hexafluoride Canister** ×2 | 2 yellowcake + 2 iron ingots + blaze powder. |
| 4 | Enrichment | **Gas Centrifuge** → **Low-Enriched** → **Highly Enriched Uranium** | Centrifuge: iron blocks, diamond, redstone, hopper, comparator. Then centrifuge + 4 UF₆ → LEU, and centrifuge + 4 LEU → HEU. The centrifuge comes back after each craft and wears out after 32 uses. |
| 5 | Metallurgy | **Enriched Uranium Ingot** | Blast furnace only. |
| 6 | Core | **Neutron Initiator** → **Fissile Core** | Initiator: gold nuggets, amethyst, glowstone. Core: 2 ingots + 6 gold ingots + the initiator. |
| 7 | Implosion | **Explosive Lens** ×2 → **Implosion Assembly** | Lens: TNT, gunpowder, glass pane, iron nugget. Assembly: 8 lenses around the core. |
| 8 | Detonator | **Detonator Circuit** → **Firing Set** | Circuit: redstone, quartz, gold, comparator, repeater. Firing set: 2 circuits, lightning rods, redstone block, iron. |
| 9 | Housing | **Casing Plate** ×2 → **Tail Fins** ×2 and **Bomb Housing** | Plates: iron block between iron ingots. Fins: 3 plates. Housing: 6 plates + 2 obsidian. |
| 10 | Assembly | **Nuclear Bomb** | Firing set on top, implosion assembly in the middle, tail fin / housing / tail fin along the bottom. |

One bomb needs about 32 raw uranium (roughly 20 ores without Fortune).

**Radioactivity:** raw uranium, yellowcake, the enriched powders, ingots, and the core are radioactive. Carrying them occasionally gives you the Radiation effect; more enriched material doses you more often. Radiation can't take you below half a heart, but it's a good reason to keep your stockpile in a chest. The UF₆ canisters are sealed and safe to carry.

**Detonating:** place the bomb, then **arm** it with flint & steel / a fire charge, or give it a redstone signal. Get well clear: the blast radius is 48 blocks.

## Building

Requires JDK 21.

```bash
./gradlew build
```

The mod jar is produced in `build/libs/`. Drop it into the `mods/` folder of a Minecraft 1.21.1 instance running NeoForge 21.1.x.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x

## Notes

Ore and item textures are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`: recolours of the matching vanilla textures (read from the NeoForge resources jar after a Gradle build) plus hand-drawn 16×16 pixel art for the machined parts. The bomb block and Radiation effect icon still use simple placeholder art.
